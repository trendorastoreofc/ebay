# Product research: 2026-09-28

Assumptions: US$1 = RM4.20. eBay fees for a non-US seller on ebay.com taken as ~13.6% final value fee + 1.65% international fee on (item + shipping) + US$0.40 per order.

## Data limits this round

eBay, Lazada, Shopee and most supplier sites were blocked from the research environment, so sold counts and sell-through rates were NOT pulled directly. Evidence comes from search-indexed eBay/Etsy/Amazon listings, supplier pages, trade press and community threads. Validate on Terapeak before buying stock (checklist at the bottom).

## Shipping change that shapes everything

- US de minimis ended for all countries on 29 Aug 2025. Standard duties apply to postal shipments from 24 Jul 2026, including a ~10% rate for Malaysia.
- Pos Malaysia **Small Packet to the US is still suspended**. US parcels go by courier (EasyParcel quotes from ~RM55, Redly Express, Aramex/Janio DDP).
- Pos Malaysia added a 40% fuel surcharge on international parcels in March 2026.
- Practical result: sub-US$40 orders to the US lose most of their margin. Target UK, Australia and the EU first, and price US listings separately with higher shipping.

## Pick 1: Malaysian batik cotton fat-quarter bundles for quilters (Score 74, Confidence Medium)

**Why this product.** Quilters treat batiks as a staple, not a trend. The US batik supply goes through a few brands (Hoffman Bali Batiks, Island Batik, Anthology), all made in Indonesia and sold at about US$4-6+ per fat quarter retail. Malaysian cap (hand-stamped) batik from Kelantan/Terengganu wholesales at RM16-20 per 2m sarong piece. One piece cuts into 6-8 fat quarters, which puts the fabric cost per FQ at roughly RM2.50-3.30. The only real work is the cutting and curation, and that is also why there is little competition.

**Target buyers.** Quilters and patchwork crafters in the UK, Australia, US and Germany. They are mostly 45+ with money to spend, loyal, and buy again when they like a supplier.

**Why demand exists.** Quilting has a very large base in the US, UK and Australia, and batik is a core category on quilting shop sites (Fat Quarter Shop, Hancock's of Paducah, batiks.com). eBay has dedicated batik fat-quarter browse categories, which means steady listing and buying activity. Demand is not seasonal. Batik is valued for its 200-220 thread count and good colour saturation.

**Why competition is still manageable.** eBay batik listings are mostly branded destash lots from US sellers and Indonesian yardage. Curated, *Malaysian-origin* cap-batik FQ bundles are rare, and big Chinese sellers don't play here because batik is hand-produced and doesn't scale in a factory. If you compete, you compete on story, curation (colourway bundles) and consistency, not on price.

**Sourcing cost (12-FQ bundle, 12 designs).**
- Fabric: ~RM36 (US$8.60)
- Packaging (ribbon, header card, mailer): ~RM4 (US$0.95)
- Weight ~550-600g packed

**Selling price.** Estimated US$34-42 for 12 FQ, modelled at US$36 + US$10 shipping (UK/AU). Unverified. Confirm on Terapeak.

**Net profit (UK/AU buyer).**
| Line | US$ |
|---|---|
| Buyer pays (item + shipping) | 46.00 |
| eBay fees (~15.25% + 0.40) | -7.40 |
| Actual shipping, Pos Small Packet + tracking, ~0.6kg (estimate RM35-45) | -9.00 |
| Fabric | -8.60 |
| Packaging | -0.95 |
| **Net** | **~20.00 (43% of revenue)** |

US buyer (charge US$15 shipping, courier ~US$14 + DDP duty ~US$5): net ~US$14 (~27%).

**Suppliers.**
1. Kelantan/Terengganu batik wholesalers (Pasar Payang, Kota Bharu, Sri Saadong Batik type sellers). Wholesale RM16-20/pc, tiered at 5 and 10 pieces.
2. Shopee/Lazada searches: "kain batik cotton asli", "batik cap Terengganu", "kain pasang batik cotton". Buy 3-4 samples from different sellers first.
3. Pemborong Kain Cotton (pemborongkaincotton.com.my) for yardage by the roll (MOQ 150m per design, only once proven).
China is not recommended. Chinese "batik" is screen-printed and kills your only differentiation.

**Risks.**
- Printed fabric being passed off as batik. Only list as "hand-stamped/cap batik" if it is. Quilters know the difference and will return or leave bad feedback.
- Colour bleed. Wax-resist batik can run. Pre-wash one piece per design before listing and say so in the listing.
- Thread-count variation between sellers. Test hand-feel and weave.
- Sarong borders (kepala) waste 15-25% of each piece.
- Labour. Cutting and bundling 10 bundles takes about 2-3 hours.
- Beginner-suitable: yes. Low capital (RM250 test), zero fragility, no batteries, no IP.

## Pick 2: Genuine TMI NH35A / NH36A automatic watch movements (Score 58, Confidence Low-Medium, conditional)

**Why this product.** The movement is made in Malaysia (Time Module / SII), and supply is tightening while prices rise. Prices went from US$30-50 a few years ago to US$60-90 for genuine units in 2025-26. Modding suppliers report "steadily increasing prices and decreasing supply". NH34 GMT jumped to US$60-100+ as Seiko prioritises internal use. A seller located where the part is made has a real angle, **if** you can get genuine stock locally below eBay prices.

**Target buyers.** Seiko modders (r/SeikoMods, WatchUSeek), microbrand builders and watch repairers in the UK, EU, Australia and the US.

**Why demand exists.** The NH35/36 is the default engine for mods and microbrands, and every build needs one. Tightening supply increases demand for sellers who have stock.

**Why competition is still manageable.** It is competitive, with established mod-parts sellers (tokeilab, namokiMODS, Parts On The Web) and HK/China sellers. It survives because the price floor is rising, not collapsing, so there is no race to the bottom. Buyers pay a premium for "genuine, not a clone" trust, and a Malaysia-based seller with rotor-stamp close-up photos can earn that trust.

**Go/no-go threshold.** Only proceed if you can buy a genuine TMI NH35A for **RM190 (US$45) or less** from a KL watch-parts wholesaler or distributor. This could not be verified this round.

**Net profit at the threshold (UK buyer).**
| Line | US$ |
|---|---|
| Buyer pays (US$75 + US$8 shipping) | 83.00 |
| eBay fees | -13.10 |
| Pos Small Packet + tracking (~100g) | -6.80 |
| Movement | -45.00 |
| **Net** | **~18.00 (22%)** |

US buyer by courier + duty: ~US$12 (13%). The weak margin is why US buyers are secondary for this product.

**Suppliers.** Watch-parts wholesalers around Jalan Petaling / Chow Kit, KL, and watch repair supply shops (ask for "TMI NH35A original, boxed"). Shopee Malaysia listings exist but many are resellers. Buy one first and verify it. Do not buy from AliExpress: that is where the clones are.

**Risks.**
- Counterfeits exist (weak rotor stamping, wrong stem). One fake sold kills your account.
- DOA/"not keeping time" claims from buyers who damaged it during install. Expect higher return exposure than fabric.
- High capital per unit and a modest margin.
- Seiko may cut third-party supply further. That is a tailwind if you hold stock, a dead end if you can't restock.
- Beginner-suitable: only after pick 1 is running.

Scores: Demand 8 | Competition 5 | Profit 5 | Sourcing 5 | Shipping 8 | Risk 5 → **58/100**
